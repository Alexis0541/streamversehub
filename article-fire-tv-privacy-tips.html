Privacy

Fire TV privacy tips

Control tracking, ads, and app permissions.

1. Review Alexa and voice settings

Turn off voice history and voice search when possible.

2. Limit ad personalization

Disable personalized ads for better privacy.

3. Check app permissions

Deny permissions not required for the app.

Install system updates for privacy and security fixes.

Further tips

Review privacy settings after updates.

  • Disable voice assistants if you don't use them regularly.
  • Use a separate profile for guests and children.
  • Remove unused apps that may collect data in the background.
