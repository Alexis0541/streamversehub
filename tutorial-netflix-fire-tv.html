How to install Netflix on your streaming device

Use the official app store for a safe installation.

On Fire TV Stick

  • Connect to the internet and open Home.
  • Search for Netflix.
  • Choose the official app and download it.
  • Open and sign in.

On an Android TV Box

  • Open Google Play Store.
  • Find the official Netflix app.
  • Install, open, and sign in.
Availability depends on region and device certification.

Tips & Troubleshooting

  • Check Wi‑Fi and reboot.
  • Keep app and system updated.
  • Use netflix.com/activate for activation codes.
  • Adjust playback quality in app settings.
