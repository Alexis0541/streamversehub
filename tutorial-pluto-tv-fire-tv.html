Pluto TV · Fire TV & TV Box

How to install Pluto TV on your streaming device

Install from the official device store.

On Fire TV Stick

  • Search for Pluto TV.
  • Download the official app.
  • Open and enjoy the app.

On an Android TV Box

  • Search in Google Play Store.
  • Confirm the official publisher.
  • Install and explore programming.
Content varies by region.

When the app does not appear

  • Retry the store search.
  • Update your device.
  • Avoid unofficial installs.
