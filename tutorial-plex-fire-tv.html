Plex · Fire TV & TV Box

How to install Plex on your streaming device

Install from the official app store.

On Fire TV Stick

  • Search for Plex.
  • Download the official app.
  • Open Plex and sign in.

On an Android TV Box

  • Search in Google Play Store.
  • Verify the official publisher.
  • Install and connect to your library.
Use a stable network connection.

When the app does not appear

  • Reload the app store and verify the publisher.
  • Verify Plex server connection.
  • Use official app listings only.
