Disney+ · Fire TV & TV Box

How to install Disney+ on your streaming device

Install the official Disney+ application.

On Fire TV Stick

  • Open search from Home.
  • Find the official Disney+ app.
  • Download and install.
  • Sign in or activate on screen.

On an Android TV Box

  • Search Google Play Store.
  • Install the official app.
  • Sign in and choose a profile.
Availability varies by country and device.

When the app does not appear

  • Update device software and retry search.
  • Confirm region and account settings.
  • Use official help pages.

Tips & Troubleshooting

  • Refresh account data.
  • Verify display and app settings.
  • Use official stores only.
