How to install YouTube on your streaming device

Install the official app from the device store.

On Fire TV Stick

  • Search for YouTube from Home.
  • Download the official YouTube app.
  • Open and sign in if needed.

On an Android TV Box

  • Open Google Play Store and search.
  • Verify the official publisher.
  • Install and open YouTube.
Check system version if needed.
