How to install Prime Video on your streaming device

Open or install Prime Video and connect your account.

On Fire TV Stick

  • Look for the included Prime Video app.
  • Search for the official app if needed.
  • Open and confirm your Amazon account.

On an Android TV Box

  • Search Google Play Store.
  • Install the official Amazon app.
  • Sign in or use the displayed registration code.
Features and availability vary by region and device.
