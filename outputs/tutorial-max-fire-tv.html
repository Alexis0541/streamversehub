How to install HBO Max on your streaming device

Install the current official app from your device store.

On Fire TV Stick

  • Open search.
  • Search for HBO Max or Max.
  • Download the official app.
  • Sign in or activate.

On an Android TV Box

  • Search Google Play Store.
  • Install the official app.
  • Open, sign in, and choose a profile.
Name and availability differ by region.
